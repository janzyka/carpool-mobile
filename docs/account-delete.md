# How to remove your account

**Effective Date**: 15.4.2026

This document describes how to remove your account from the Jedu application and delete all associated data. By following these steps, you will ensure that your personal information is no longer stored in our system.

## Delete your account

To delete your account, follow these steps:

- send an email to jedu.jedes@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and your phone number in the body of the email
- you will receive confirmation SMS message asking you to confirm the deletion by entering the confirmation code
- send email to jedu.jedes@gmail.com with the subject "Delete account" and the phone number and confirmation code in the body of the email
- your account will be deleted and all your data will be removed from our system
